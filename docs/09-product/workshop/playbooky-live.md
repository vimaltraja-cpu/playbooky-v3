# Purpose

PlayBooky Live is the live facilitation environment used to deliver workshops.

It transforms a prepared workshop into an interactive experience that supports facilitators and participants throughout the session.

PlayBooky Live is the final stage of the PlayBooky Product System.

---

# Responsibilities

PlayBooky Live is responsible for:

- Guiding facilitators through the workshop.
- Presenting activities in the correct sequence.
- Supporting participant engagement.
- Displaying activity instructions.
- Supporting AI-assisted facilitation.
- Capturing workshop progress.
- Providing a seamless delivery experience.

PlayBooky Live does not create workshops.

It delivers workshops created by the Workshop Builder.

---

# Inputs

Consumes:

- Workshop Builder.
- Facilitator Guide.
- Activity Library.
- Prompt Library.

---

# Outputs

Produces:

- Live workshop experience.
- Workshop progress.
- Facilitation events.
- Future analytics data.

---

# Delivery Principles

PlayBooky Live follows these principles.

- The facilitator remains in control.
- Guidance should appear only when needed.
- Activities should feel effortless to run.
- AI should support facilitation without becoming the facilitator.
- Delivery should adapt without disrupting the workshop.
- The experience should remain calm, focused and unobtrusive.

---

# Relationship to the Product System

Consumes:

- Workshop Builder.
- Facilitator Guide.

Produces:

- Live workshop delivery.

PlayBooky Live represents the final operational layer of the PlayBooky platform.
