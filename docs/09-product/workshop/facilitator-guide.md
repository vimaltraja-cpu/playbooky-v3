# Purpose

The Facilitator Guide transforms a completed workshop into a facilitator-ready experience.

It provides everything required to confidently prepare for and deliver a workshop, bringing together workshop structure, activity guidance, prompts and facilitation advice into a single reference.

The Facilitator Guide bridges workshop design and workshop delivery.

---

# Responsibilities

The Facilitator Guide is responsible for:

- Presenting the complete workshop agenda.
- Providing activity-by-activity facilitation guidance.
- Providing preparation advice.
- Providing workshop timings.
- Providing facilitator notes.
- Providing AI-generated support where appropriate.
- Preparing facilitators for live delivery.

The Facilitator Guide does not modify the workshop.

It represents the final preparation stage before facilitation.

# Guide Principles

The Facilitator Guide follows these principles.

- Guidance should reduce facilitator preparation.
- Information should be organised chronologically.
- Activities should remain easy to understand.
- AI should enhance preparation without replacing facilitator judgement.
- Preparation should remain focused on workshop delivery.
