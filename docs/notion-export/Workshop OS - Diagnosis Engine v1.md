# Workshop OS - Diagnosis Engine v1

## Purpose

The Diagnosis Engine is the intelligence layer of Workshop OS.

Its purpose is to understand the user's situation before a workshop is designed.

Rather than selecting from predefined templates, Workshop OS diagnoses the challenge, identifies the current stage of thinking, and determines what type of workshop activities are most likely to help the team move forward.

The diagnosis engine is responsible for identifying:

- Primary Stage
- Secondary Stage
- Desired Outcome
- Constraints
- Workshop Design Requirements

---

# Core Principle

The diagnosis engine does not ask:

"What workshop do you want to run?"

Instead it asks:

"What challenge are you trying to solve?"

The goal is to understand:

- Where the team is today
- What they already know
- What they need next
- What outcome they are trying to achieve

---

# Diagnosis Inputs

The diagnosis engine collects information across five areas.

---

## 1. Challenge

Question:

What challenge are you trying to solve?

Examples:

- User drop-off
- Poor onboarding
- Stakeholder disagreement
- Prioritisation issues
- Product strategy
- Process inefficiencies
- Team alignment
- Feature planning

Purpose:

Identify the primary problem space.

---

## 2. Current State

Question:

What do you already know?

Examples:

- Research completed
- Research partially completed
- No research available
- Opportunities identified
- Solutions already proposed
- Decisions still required

Purpose:

Understand the team's current level of certainty.

---

## 3. Desired Outcome

Question:

What do you need by the end of the workshop?

Examples:

- Shared understanding
- Prioritised opportunities
- Clear decision
- Defined roadmap
- Defined MVP
- Action plan
- Stakeholder alignment

Purpose:

Identify the target outcome.

---

## 4. Constraints

Questions:

How much time is available?

How many participants will attend?

Will the workshop be remote or in person?

Will senior stakeholders attend?

Will the workshop be cross-functional?

Examples:

- 30 minutes
- 60 minutes
- 90 minutes
- Half day
- Full day

Purpose:

Ensure generated workshops are realistic.

---

## 5. Blockers

Question:

What is currently preventing progress?

Examples:

- Lack of evidence
- Too many assumptions
- Too many ideas
- Lack of alignment
- Unclear ownership
- Lack of prioritisation
- No clear direction

Purpose:

Identify the biggest obstacle preventing movement.

---

# Stage Identification

The diagnosis engine determines both a Primary Stage and a Secondary Stage.

The Primary Stage represents the team's biggest need.

The Secondary Stage represents the next likely step after the primary challenge has been addressed.

---

# Stage Definitions

## Goals

Question being answered:

What are we trying to achieve?

Signals:

- No shared vision
- No shared objectives
- No success criteria
- Conflicting priorities
- Lack of direction

Expected Outcomes:

- Shared objective
- Clear success measures
- Aligned priorities

---

## Understand

Question being answered:

What is really happening?

Signals:

- Need research
- Need evidence
- Need user understanding
- Need process understanding
- High uncertainty
- Heavy reliance on assumptions

Expected Outcomes:

- Better understanding
- Evidence
- Shared knowledge

---

## Frame

Question being answered:

How should we define the challenge?

Signals:

- Problem unclear
- Problem too broad
- Multiple interpretations
- No agreed problem statement

Expected Outcomes:

- Defined challenge
- Clear scope
- Shared framing

---

## Ideas

Question being answered:

What could we do?

Signals:

- Need solutions
- Need alternatives
- Need innovation
- Need concepts

Expected Outcomes:

- New ideas
- Potential solutions
- Opportunity areas

---

## Evaluate

Question being answered:

Which options are strongest?

Signals:

- Too many ideas
- Too many opportunities
- Need prioritisation
- Need comparison
- Need trade-off discussions

Expected Outcomes:

- Prioritised opportunities
- Ranked options
- Clear recommendations

---

## Decide

Question being answered:

What should we commit to?

Signals:

- Need ownership
- Need commitment
- Need roadmap
- Need action plan
- Need decision making

Expected Outcomes:

- Decisions
- Actions
- Ownership
- Commitments

---

## Discuss

Question being answered:

How do we align perspectives?

Signals:

- Stakeholder disagreement
- Misalignment
- Communication issues
- Political tension
- Differing viewpoints

Expected Outcomes:

- Alignment
- Shared understanding
- Improved communication

---

# Diagnosis Outputs

The diagnosis engine produces:

## Primary Stage

The stage requiring the greatest attention.

Example:

Evaluate

---

## Secondary Stage

The stage most likely to follow the primary stage.

Example:

Decide

---

## Confidence Score

How confident the system is in its diagnosis.

Example:

High

Medium

Low

---

## Workshop Design Requirements

The requirements used by the Workshop Design Engine.

Examples:

- Workshop duration
- Participant count
- Remote/In Person
- Stakeholder involvement
- Desired outcome

---

# Example Diagnosis

Input:

Research completed.

15 opportunities identified.

Need stakeholder alignment.

Need prioritisation.

90-minute workshop.

Output:

Primary Stage:

Evaluate

Secondary Stage:

Decide

Confidence:

High

Workshop Requirements:

90 minutes

8 participants

Remote

Cross-functional

Prioritisation and decision making

---

# Future Vision

The diagnosis engine will become the core intelligence layer of Workshop OS.

Its role is not to recommend predefined workshops.

Its role is to understand the user's situation and provide the Workshop Design Engine with the information required to generate a bespoke workshop.
