# Review Objectives

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Do not debate which objective is best at this stage.

Focus only on understanding and clarification.
Instructions: Review each submitted objective one at a time.

Clarify wording where needed.

Ensure participants understand what each objective is trying to achieve before moving on.
Parent Block: Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) (https://app.notion.com/p/Objectives-and-Key-Results-OKRs-373eb253b7f68077acd4f9dbe3b761bc?pvs=21)
Purpose: Review proposed objectives and ensure everyone understands the options being considered.
Step Order: 2
