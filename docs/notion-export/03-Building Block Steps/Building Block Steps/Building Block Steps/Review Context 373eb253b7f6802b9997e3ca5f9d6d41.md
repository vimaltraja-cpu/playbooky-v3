# Review Context

Duration: 10
Facilitator Notes: Keep the discussion focused on the agreed topic.

Avoid moving into solutions at this stage.
Instructions: Introduce the focus area being reviewed.

Explain the purpose of the activity.

Review any relevant context, evidence or observations.

Ensure everyone understands the scope before beginning.
Parent Block: Start Stop Continue (https://app.notion.com/p/Start-Stop-Continue-373eb253b7f680b08fb3da2d5df49b4e?pvs=21)
Purpose: Create a shared understanding of the topic, process or team behaviour being reviewed.
Step Order: 1
