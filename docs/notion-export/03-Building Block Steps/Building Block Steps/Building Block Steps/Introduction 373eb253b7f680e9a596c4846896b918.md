# Introduction

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Keep the introduction short and focused.

Avoid teaching OKRs in depth.

The goal is to create enough understanding for participants to confidently take part in the activity.
Instructions: Introduce the purpose of the workshop.

Explain the difference between Objectives and Key Results.

Set expectations for the session and explain that the goal is to align around a single objective and measurable indicators of success.

Review the agenda and answer any questions before beginning.
Parent Block: Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) (https://app.notion.com/p/Objectives-and-Key-Results-OKRs-373eb253b7f68077acd4f9dbe3b761bc?pvs=21)
Purpose: Create a shared understanding of the workshop objective and explain how OKRs will be used during the session.
