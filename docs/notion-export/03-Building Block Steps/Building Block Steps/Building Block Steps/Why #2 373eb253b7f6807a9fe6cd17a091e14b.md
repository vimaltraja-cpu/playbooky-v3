# Why #2

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Keep digging beneath symptoms.

Challenge assumptions and ask for evidence where possible.

Avoid accepting the first answer without discussion.

The objective is to move closer to the root cause.
Instructions: Take the agreed cause from Why #1.

Ask the group:

"Why is this happening?"

Capture responses.

Discuss the responses and agree on the most likely underlying cause.

Record the agreed cause so it can be explored further in the next step.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Explore the underlying cause of the first identified cause.
Step Order: 3
