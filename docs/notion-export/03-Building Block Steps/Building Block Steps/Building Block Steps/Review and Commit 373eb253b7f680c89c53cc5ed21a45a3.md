# Review and Commit

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Do not end the workshop without clear ownership.

Ensure actions are assigned to individuals rather than teams.

Finish with clarity on what happens next and when progress will be reviewed.
Instructions: Review the final Objective and Key Results.

Agree who owns each Key Result.

Capture any follow-up actions and review dates.

Confirm commitment from the group before closing the activity.
Parent Block: Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) (https://app.notion.com/p/Objectives-and-Key-Results-OKRs-373eb253b7f68077acd4f9dbe3b761bc?pvs=21)
Purpose: Confirm alignment on the final Objective and Key Results and agree ownership.
