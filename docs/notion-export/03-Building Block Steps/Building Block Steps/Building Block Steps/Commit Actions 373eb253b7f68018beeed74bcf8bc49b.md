# Commit Actions

Duration: 10
Facilitator Notes: Do not end the activity without clear ownership.

Ensure actions are assigned to individuals and have realistic deadlines.
Instructions: Review the selected priorities.

Agree actions, owners and timelines.

Capture next steps and confirm accountability.
Parent Block: Priority Map (https://app.notion.com/p/Priority-Map-373eb253b7f6802eb4fae1026a89a2c8?pvs=21)
Purpose: Capture actions, ownership and next steps for the selected priorities.
Step Order: 6
Technique Used: Who, What, When (https://app.notion.com/p/Who-What-When-373eb253b7f68060a116d23c05af6816?pvs=21)
