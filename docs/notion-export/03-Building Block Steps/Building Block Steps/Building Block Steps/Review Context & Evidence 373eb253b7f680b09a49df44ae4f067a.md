# Review Context & Evidence

Duration: 10
Facilitator Notes: Focus on facts and evidence rather than opinions.

Avoid discussing solutions.

The goal is to establish a shared understanding of the situation.
Instructions: Review the available research, insights, feedback and evidence.

Present key findings and observations.

Allow participants to ask clarifying questions.

Ensure everyone has a common understanding of the challenge before defining the problem.
Parent Block: Problem Statement (https://app.notion.com/p/Problem-Statement-373eb253b7f680faa68af78e68f8ce42?pvs=21)
Purpose: Create a shared understanding of the evidence, research and observations that led to the workshop.
Step Order: 1
