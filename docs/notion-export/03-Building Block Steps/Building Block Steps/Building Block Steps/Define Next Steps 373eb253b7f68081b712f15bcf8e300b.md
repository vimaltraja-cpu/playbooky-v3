# Define Next Steps

Duration: 10
Facilitator Notes: Five Whys should rarely end without action.

Ensure the outputs are carried into the next activity, such as Problem Statement, How Might We or Opportunity Mapping.
Instructions: Review the identified root causes.

Agree any immediate actions, owners and follow-up activities.

Capture next steps and ensure accountability before closing the activity.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Agree how the root causes will be used to inform future activities or decisions.
Step Order: 8
Technique Used: Who, What, When (https://app.notion.com/p/Who-What-When-373eb253b7f68060a116d23c05af6816?pvs=21)
