# Select Priorities

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Use voting to reduce bias and avoid dominant voices driving the decision.

Focus on commitment rather than perfection.
Instructions: Review the opportunities identified as strong candidates.

Ask participants to vote independently.

Reveal votes simultaneously.

Identify the highest-priority opportunities.
Parent Block: Impact Effort Map (https://app.notion.com/p/Impact-Effort-Map-373eb253b7f680129bb0fbbd614dbd2e?pvs=21)
Purpose: Align on which opportunities should move forward.
Step Order: 5
Technique Used: Blind Vote (https://app.notion.com/p/Blind-Vote-373eb253b7f680fdb80effd9c0fd2384?pvs=21)
