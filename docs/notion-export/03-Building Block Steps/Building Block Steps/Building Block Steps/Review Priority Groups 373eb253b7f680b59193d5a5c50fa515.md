# Review Priority Groups

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Look for clusters and themes rather than focusing on individual items.

Encourage the team to discuss what the prioritisation is revealing.
Instructions: Review the prioritised opportunities.

Group similar priorities together where appropriate.

Discuss patterns, dependencies and strategic themes.

Identify which opportunities appear most important.
Parent Block: Priority Map (https://app.notion.com/p/Priority-Map-373eb253b7f6802eb4fae1026a89a2c8?pvs=21)
Purpose: Review the emerging priority groups and identify patterns in the prioritisation.
Step Order: 4
Technique Used: Theme Sort (https://app.notion.com/p/Theme-Sort-373eb253b7f680a79694c82252566fab?pvs=21)
