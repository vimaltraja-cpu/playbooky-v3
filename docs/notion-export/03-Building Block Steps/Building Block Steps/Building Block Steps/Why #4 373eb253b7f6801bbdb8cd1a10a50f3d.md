# Why #4

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: By this stage the group should be moving beyond symptoms and into organisational, process or behavioural causes.

Keep asking for evidence and examples.
Instructions: Take the agreed cause from Why #3.

Ask the group:

"Why is this happening?"

Capture responses.

Discuss the responses and agree on the most likely underlying cause.

Record the agreed cause so it can be explored further in the next step.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Explore deeper systemic causes and identify factors contributing to the problem.
Step Order: 5
