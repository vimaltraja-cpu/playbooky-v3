# Map Opportunities

Duration: 15
Facilitator Notes: Focus on relative comparison rather than perfect scoring.

Encourage evidence-based discussion where possible.

Avoid spending too much time on individual opportunities.
Instructions: Review each opportunity one at a time.

Discuss expected impact.

Discuss expected effort.

Place the opportunity on the matrix.

Repeat until all opportunities have been mapped.
Parent Block: Impact Effort Map (https://app.notion.com/p/Impact-Effort-Map-373eb253b7f680129bb0fbbd614dbd2e?pvs=21)
Purpose: Evaluate and position opportunities based on their expected impact and effort.
Step Order: 3
