# Why #1

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Encourage participants to use evidence and observations where possible.

Avoid jumping to solutions.

Focus on understanding causes rather than assigning blame.
Instructions: Ask the group:

"Why is this problem happening?"

Capture all responses.

Discuss the responses and agree on the most likely cause.

Record the agreed cause clearly as it will become the input for the next why.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Identify the most immediate cause of the problem.
Step Order: 2
