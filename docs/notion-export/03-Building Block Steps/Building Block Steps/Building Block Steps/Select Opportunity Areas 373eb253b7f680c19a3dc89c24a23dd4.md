# Select Opportunity Areas

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: The objective is prioritisation, not perfection.

If there is no clear winner, facilitate a short discussion and vote again.
Instructions: Review the shortlisted opportunity areas.

Ask participants to vote independently.

Reveal votes simultaneously.

Select the highest scoring opportunities for future exploration.
Parent Block: How Might We (https://app.notion.com/p/How-Might-We-373eb253b7f6800ab71de85617ce4030?pvs=21)
Purpose: Align on the opportunity areas that should be explored further.
Step Order: 5
Technique Used: Blind Vote (https://app.notion.com/p/Blind-Vote-373eb253b7f680fdb80effd9c0fd2384?pvs=21)
