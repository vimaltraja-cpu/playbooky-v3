# Refine Opportunity Areas

Duration: 10
Facilitator Notes: Focus on opportunity quality rather than quantity.

Avoid moving into solution discussions.

Ensure the opportunities remain connected to the original problem statement.
Instructions: Review each opportunity cluster.

Discuss overlaps and similarities.

Merge related opportunities where appropriate.

Create a shortlist of the strongest opportunity areas.
Parent Block: How Might We (https://app.notion.com/p/How-Might-We-373eb253b7f6800ab71de85617ce4030?pvs=21)
Purpose: Refine the strongest opportunity areas and remove duplicates or overlapping themes.
Step Order: 4
