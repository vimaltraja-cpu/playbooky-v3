# Why #3

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Watch for participants repeating previous answers in different words.

Encourage the group to think about systems, processes and behaviours.

If the discussion moves towards solutions, redirect the conversation back to causes.
Instructions: Take the agreed cause from Why #2.

Ask the group:

"Why is this happening?"

Capture responses.

Discuss the responses and agree on the most likely underlying cause.

Record the agreed cause so it can be explored further in the next step.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Continue exploring deeper causes to move beyond surface-level explanations.
Step Order: 4
