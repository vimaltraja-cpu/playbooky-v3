# Why #5

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Do not force the group to stop at exactly five levels if a root cause has already emerged.

Equally, do not stop too early if the group is still discussing symptoms.
Instructions: Take the agreed cause from Why #4.

Ask the group:

"Why is this happening?"

Capture responses.

Discuss the responses and agree on the most likely root cause.

Record the final root cause clearly and visibly.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Identify the root cause or causes that the group believes are driving the problem.
Step Order: 6
