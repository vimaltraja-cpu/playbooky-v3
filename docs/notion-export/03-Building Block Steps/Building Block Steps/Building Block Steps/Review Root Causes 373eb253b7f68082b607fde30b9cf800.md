# Review Root Causes

Duration: 5
Facilitator Notes: Look for gaps in logic.

Challenge conclusions that are not supported by evidence.

It is acceptable to identify multiple root causes if they are connected.
Instructions: Review each why and its corresponding answer.

Walk the group through the full chain of reasoning.

Confirm agreement on the root cause or causes before moving on.
Parent Block: Five Whys (https://app.notion.com/p/Five-Whys-373eb253b7f68085bcc4f82ab630e055?pvs=21)
Purpose: Review the chain of causes and confirm the root cause or causes identified by the group.
Step Order: 7
