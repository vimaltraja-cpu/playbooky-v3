# Generate Success Metrics

Duration: 10
Facilitator Notes: Keep participants focused on measurable signals rather than solutions or outputs.

Push for observable outcomes where possible.
Instructions: Ask participants:

How would we know we are making progress?

Capture every suggestion.

Encourage quantity over quality.

Avoid evaluating metrics while they are being generated.
Parent Block: Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) (https://app.notion.com/p/Objectives-and-Key-Results-OKRs-373eb253b7f68077acd4f9dbe3b761bc?pvs=21)
Purpose: Generate possible measures that indicate progress towards the selected objective.
Step Order: 5
