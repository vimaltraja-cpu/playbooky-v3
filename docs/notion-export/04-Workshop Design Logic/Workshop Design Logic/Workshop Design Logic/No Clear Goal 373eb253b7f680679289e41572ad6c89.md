# No Clear Goal

Expected Output: Key results, Objective, Success metrics
Reason: Teams need a clear objective before they can effectively prioritise, plan, evaluate or execute work. Establishing a shared goal creates alignment and provides a foundation for future workshop activities.
Recommended Building Block: Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) (https://app.notion.com/p/Objectives-and-Key-Results-OKRs-373eb253b7f68077acd4f9dbe3b761bc?pvs=21)
Situation: The team cannot clearly describe what success looks like, what they are trying to achieve, or how success will be measured.
What Next?: How Might We (https://app.notion.com/p/How-Might-We-373eb253b7f6800ab71de85617ce4030?pvs=21), Priority Map (https://app.notion.com/p/Priority-Map-373eb253b7f6802eb4fae1026a89a2c8?pvs=21), Start Stop Continue (https://app.notion.com/p/Start-Stop-Continue-373eb253b7f680b08fb3da2d5df49b4e?pvs=21)
