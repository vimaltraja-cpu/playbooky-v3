# Service Map

Best Used When: The team needs to understand operational complexity.
Duration: 90 mins
Facilitator Notes: Keep participants focused on the whole service rather than isolated touchpoints.

Look for bottlenecks, handoffs and operational complexity.

Encourage contributions from multiple disciplines.
Inputs Required: Existing research, Stakeholders
Instructions: 1. Define the service being explored.

2. Identify the key stages of the experience.

3. Map customer actions.

4. Map supporting processes and systems.

5. Identify dependencies and pain points.

6. Discuss opportunities for improvement.
Layout Type: Map
Outputs Produced: Alignment, Insights
Purpose: Visualise people, systems and processes involved in delivering a service.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Understand
