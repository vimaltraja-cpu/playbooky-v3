# Crazy Eights

Best Used When: The team needs options, concepts, or alternative approaches.
Duration: 30 mins
Facilitator Notes: Emphasise speed over quality.

Encourage participants to avoid self-editing.

The goal is variety and exploration rather than polished ideas.
Inputs Required: Opportunities, Problem statement
Instructions: 1. Present the challenge or opportunity.

2. Divide paper or canvas into eight sections.

3. Give participants one minute per section.

4. Generate one idea in each section.

5. Continue until all eight spaces are complete.

6. Share ideas with the group.

7. Discuss promising concepts.
Layout Type: Canvas
Outputs Produced: Ideas
Purpose: Generate a range of solution ideas quickly.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Ideas
