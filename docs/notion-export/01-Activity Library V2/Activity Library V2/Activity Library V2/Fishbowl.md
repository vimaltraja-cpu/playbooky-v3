# Fishbowl

Best Used When: The group has differing viewpoints or sensitive topics need discussion.
Duration: 60 mins
Facilitator Notes: Ensure balanced participation.

Avoid allowing a small number of people to dominate.

Focus on listening and understanding rather than winning arguments.
Inputs Required: Problem statement, Stakeholders
Instructions: 1. Select a small group to begin the discussion.

2. Remaining participants observe and listen.

3. Participants rotate into the discussion as needed.

4. Capture key insights and themes.

5. Discuss areas of agreement and disagreement.

6. Summarise findings.
Layout Type: Discussion
Outputs Produced: Alignment, Insights
Purpose: Enable structured discussion around difficult topics while ensuring all perspectives are heard.
Remote Friendly: Hybrid
Stage: Discuss
