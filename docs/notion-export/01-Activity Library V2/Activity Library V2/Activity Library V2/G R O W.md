# G.R.O.W

Best Used When: The team needs clarity on direction, objectives or next steps.
Duration: 60 mins
Facilitator Notes: Keep the conversation grounded in reality.

Avoid jumping straight to solutions.

Spend sufficient time exploring the current situation before discussing options.

Ensure the session ends with clear commitments and actions.
Inputs Required: Business goal, Team context
Instructions: 1. Define the Goal the team wants to achieve.

2. Explore the current Reality of the situation.

3. Generate Options for moving forward.

4. Discuss the pros and cons of each option.

5. Agree on the Way Forward.

6. Capture actions, owners and timelines.
Layout Type: Rows
Outputs Produced: Action plan, Shared goal
Purpose: Help individuals or teams define goals, understand reality, explore options and agree actions.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Goals
