# Mind Map

Best Used When: The team needs a broad range of possibilities.
Duration: 30 mins
Facilitator Notes: Encourage quantity over quality initially.

Avoid judging ideas during generation.

Look for clusters and connections that may not be obvious.

Use the final discussion to identify promising areas for further exploration.
Inputs Required: Problem statement
Instructions: 1. Write the challenge in the centre of the canvas.

2. Individually generate related ideas, themes or opportunities.

3. Add branches from the central challenge.

4. Continue expanding branches with supporting ideas.

5. Review and discuss emerging themes.

6. Identify areas worth exploring further.
Layout Type: Map
Outputs Produced: Ideas
Purpose: Generate ideas through associative thinking.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Ideas
