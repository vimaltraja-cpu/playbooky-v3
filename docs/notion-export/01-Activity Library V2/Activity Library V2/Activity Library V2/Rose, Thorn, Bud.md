# Rose, Thorn, Bud

Best Used When: The team wants a balanced evaluation of a project, feature, process or experience.
Duration: 30 mins
Facilitator Notes: Encourage balanced thinking.

Prevent the conversation from becoming overly negative.

Use the opportunity section to identify future actions.
Inputs Required: Existing research, Ideas, User feedback
Instructions: 1. Create three columns: Rose, Thorn and Bud.

2. Ask participants to identify positive elements.

3. Capture challenges and pain points.

4. Identify emerging opportunities.

5. Discuss patterns and themes.

6. Agree on actions or areas for further exploration.
Layout Type: Columns
Outputs Produced: Ideas, Insights
Purpose: Identify positives, challenges and opportunities within a situation.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Evaluate
