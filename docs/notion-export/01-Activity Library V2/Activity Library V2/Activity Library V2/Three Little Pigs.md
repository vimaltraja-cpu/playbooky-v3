# Three Little Pigs

Best Used When: The team needs to evaluate whether solutions are fragile, developing or robust.
Duration: 60 mins
Facilitator Notes: Focus discussion on evidence rather than opinion.

Challenge assumptions.

Use the exercise to identify risk and increase confidence in decision making.
Inputs Required: Ideas, Opportunities, Stakeholders
Instructions: 1. Create three categories: Straw House, Stick House and Brick House.

2. Review ideas, plans or processes.

3. Place each item into the appropriate category.

4. Discuss reasons for placement.

5. Identify improvements required to strengthen weaker items.

6. Agree priorities and actions.
Layout Type: Columns
Outputs Produced: Decision, Insights
Purpose: Assess ideas, processes or initiatives based on confidence and maturity.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Discuss
