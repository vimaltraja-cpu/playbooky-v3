# Blind Vote

Best Used When: The team needs to choose between options fairly and quickly.
Duration: 5 mins
Facilitator Notes: Use when dominant personalities may influence decisions.

Avoid debating options during voting.

The objective is to reveal genuine preferences.
Inputs Required: Ideas, Opportunities
Instructions: 1. Present the available options.

2. Ensure participants understand each option.

3. Ask participants to vote privately.

4. Collect votes simultaneously.

5. Reveal results.

6. Discuss outcomes if necessary.

7. Confirm the final decision.
Layout Type: Voting
Outputs Produced: Decision
Purpose: Help the group make a quick decision without influence from louder voices.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Decide
