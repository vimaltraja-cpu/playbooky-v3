# Heart, Head, Hand

Best Used When: The team wants balanced decision making and needs to consider multiple viewpoints.
Duration: 30 mins
Facilitator Notes: Encourage participants to contribute to all three areas.

Watch for teams focusing too heavily on logic and ignoring emotional or practical factors.

Use the discussion to reveal hidden trade-offs.
Inputs Required: Ideas, Opportunities
Outputs Produced: Decision, Insights
Purpose: Evaluate ideas from emotional, logical and practical perspectives.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Evaluate
