# Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)

Activity Outcomes: Alignment, Commitment, Goal setting, Strategy definition, Success measurement
Best Used When: The team has competing priorities, unclear success measures, multiple stakeholder opinions, or lacks a shared direction. Useful for quarterly planning, strategy workshops and project kick-offs.
Complexity: Medium
Duration: 60 mins
Energy Level: Medium
Facilitator Notes: Push for a single objective.

Challenge vague wording.

Avoid discussing solutions.

Focus on outcomes rather than outputs.

Encourage ambitious but realistic key results.

Keep debate time-boxed.

Use voting to break deadlocks.

Good Output Looks Like: One agreed objective.

Three measurable key results.

Named owners.

Review cadence agreed.

Clear understanding of what success looks like.
If Running Long: Reduce discussion time.

Move to voting sooner.

Capture unresolved points in a parking lot.

Use facilitator decision-making if alignment cannot be reached within the allocated time.

If metric generation is running long, stop at the top five metrics and move into prioritisation.
Inputs Required: Business goal, Stakeholders, Team context
Layout Type: Hybrid
Outputs Produced: Alignment, Key results, Shared goal, Success metrics
Preparation Needed: Before the workshop ask participants to submit one proposed objective.Prepare a board with:- Objective submission area- Objective clustering area- Voting area- Metrics generation area- Final OKR area- Actions and owners areaEnsure everyone understands the purpose of the session before joining.
Purpose: Create alignment around a shared objective and define measurable indicators of success.
Recommended Participants: 4–12 participants
Remote Friendly: Hybrid
Stage: Goals
Step-by-Step Flow: Step 1 – Introduction

Explain the purpose of the session.

Introduce Objectives and Key Results.

Explain that the goal is to align around one primary objective and define measurable indicators of success.

---

Step 2 – Review Objectives

Review all submitted objectives.

Ensure everyone understands each proposal.

Clarify wording where necessary.

---

Step 3 – Theme Sort Objectives

Group similar objectives together.

Identify common themes.

Discuss overlaps and differences.

---

Step 4 – Select Objective

Discuss the strongest options.

Run a blind vote.

If necessary conduct a second round.

Agree one primary objective.

---

Step 5 – Generate Success Metrics

Ask participants:

How would we know we are making progress?

Capture all suggested metrics.

Focus on quantity over quality initially.

---

Step 6 – Theme Sort Metrics

Group similar metrics.

Remove duplicates.

Identify the strongest indicators of success.

---

Step 7 – Select Metrics

Use dot voting or blind voting.

Select the three most meaningful metrics.

---

Step 8 – Create Key Results

Convert each metric into a measurable target.

Example:

Metric:
Customer retention

Key Result:
Increase customer retention from 62% to 75%.

---

Step 9 – Review and Commit

Review the final objective.

Review all key results.

Confirm ownership.

Agree review cadence and next steps.
Techniques Used: Blind vote, Clustering, Discussion, Dot voting, Theme Sort
