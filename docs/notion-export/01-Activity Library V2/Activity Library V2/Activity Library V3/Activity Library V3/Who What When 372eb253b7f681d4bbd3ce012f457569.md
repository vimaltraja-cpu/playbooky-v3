# Who What When

Best Used When: The team has made a decision and needs to agree next steps.
Duration: 10 mins
Facilitator Notes: Do not leave actions unassigned.

Push for clear ownership.

Ensure deadlines are realistic.

End the workshop with visible accountability.
Inputs Required: Decision, Stakeholders
Outputs Produced: Action plan
Purpose: Turn decisions into clear ownership, actions, and timings.
Remote Friendly: Yes
Stage: Decide
